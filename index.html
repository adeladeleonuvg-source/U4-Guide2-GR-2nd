Unit 4: Food & Polite Requests (Our World 2)

Welcome to our interactive grammar whiteboard! In Unit 4, we explore two essential grammar structures to talk about food, quantities, and polite requests in English.

Grammar I: Questions & Answers with any

We use Is there any... ? for singular/uncountable nouns, and Are there any... ? for plural countable nouns.

Singular / Uncountable:

Q: Is there any pasta / meat / corn?
A (+): Yes, there is.
A (-): No, there isn't any pasta.

Plural Countable:

Q: Are there any tomatoes / peppers / eggs?
A (+): Yes, there are.
A (-): No, there aren't any tomatoes.

Grammar II: Polite Requests with May

We use May I have... , please? when we want to ask politely for food or drinks.

Structure Formula:

May + I + have + [some + food] + please?

Example Answers:

• Yes. Sure.
• Yes, here you are.
• Not now. Dinner is at 6:00.

Practice Exercises (15 Total)

Solve each problem on your whiteboard notebook, then click "Show Solution" to check your work!

⭐ Basic Level Exercises (1 to 5)

1. Fill in the blank: Is there ______ pasta?

Step-by-step solution: "pasta" is an uncountable noun used in singular questions with any.
Answer: Is there any pasta?

2. Fill in the blank: Are there ______ tomatoes?

Step-by-step solution: "tomatoes" is a plural countable noun, so we use Are there any.
Answer: Are there any tomatoes?

3. Answer based on the chart: Is there any corn? (Negative answer)

Step-by-step solution: For a negative singular answer with corn, use "isn't any".
Answer: No, there isn't any corn.

4. Answer based on the chart: Are there any peppers? (Negative answer)

Step-by-step solution: For a negative plural answer with peppers, use "aren't any".
Answer: No, there aren't any peppers.

5. Order the words to make a polite request: have / cheese, / I / please / May / some / ?

Step-by-step solution: Start with "May", then pronoun "I", verb "have", quantity "some", object "cheese", and polite word "please?".
Answer: May I have some cheese, please?
⭐⭐ Intermediate Level Exercises (6 to 10)

6. Order words: some / we / May / milk, / have / please / ?

Step-by-step solution: Modal "May" + pronoun "we" + verb "have" + "some milk" + "please?".
Answer: May we have some milk, please?

7. Look at the kitchen counter: Are there ______ eggs? (Eggs are present)

Step-by-step solution: Use "any" for the question and "Yes, there are." for the affirmative response.
Answer: Are there any eggs? Yes, there are.

8. Look at the kitchen counter: Is there ______ chicken? (Chicken is present)

Step-by-step solution: Use "any" with singular uncountable chicken and affirm.
Answer: Is there any chicken? Yes, there is.

9. Complete listening sentence: "______ we have some bread, please? Not right now."

Step-by-step solution: Polite requests starting with a modal verb for permission begin with May.
Answer: May we have some bread, please?

10. Order words: ice cream, / we / please / have / May / some / ?

Step-by-step solution: Order components: May + we + have + some ice cream + please?
Answer: May we have some ice cream, please?
⭐⭐⭐ Advanced Level Exercises (11 to 15)

12. Categorize "fish" into the correct chart question header.

Answer: Is there any corn? No, there isn't any corn.

Step-by-step solution: May + I + have + some + carrots + please?

Critical Thinking & Logic

Question 1: Singular vs. Plural

Question 2: Polite Manners

Why is it important to use "May I have... , please?" instead of demanding food directly? How does tone of voice change politeness in different cultures?
